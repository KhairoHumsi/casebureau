Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Applies to the Case Bureau app, the YouTube channel “The Case Bureau” and the Instagram account of The Case Bureau

Who we are

The Case Bureau app (“the app”) is a private tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel “The Case Bureau”. It is used only by the channel owner to manage that channel and its Instagram account. Contact: khair0.humsi@gmail.com.

Google and YouTube data the app uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channel, and update their titles, descriptions and privacy settings (scope youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl);
  • read the owner's channel details and video list (youtube.readonly);
  • read public comments and replies on the owner's own videos, including commenters' public display names (youtube.force-ssl);
  • read the owner's channel analytics, such as views and audience activity by time (yt-analytics.readonly).

The app does not access any other person's account and does not collect passwords.

Instagram and Facebook data the app uses

The app is a Meta developer app (“Case Bureau Publisher”) connected only to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account. With the owner's permission it uses the Instagram Graph API to:

  • publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram account (instagram_content_publish, instagram_basic);
  • read public comments on the owner's own posts, including commenters' public usernames, to summarize viewer theories (instagram_manage_comments);
  • read the owner's own post insights, such as views and reach (instagram_manage_insights);
  • find the owner's Page and the Instagram account linked to it (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management).

The app never logs in other users, never sends messages, and never accesses anyone else's account.

How the data is used

Data is used only to run the channel: publishing episodes, reviewing viewer theories about a case, and choosing publishing times. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except as needed to operate the channel through Google's and Meta's own services.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Sign-in tokens and downloaded comments are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, with file permissions limited to that user. Comment copies for a case are deleted seven days after that case's solution episode is published. Sign-in tokens are kept until access is revoked.

Data deletion

To have any data about you removed (for example a copy of a comment you posted), email khair0.humsi@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion” and your YouTube or Instagram username. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Copies of comments are also deleted automatically seven days after the case's solution is published. The account owner can remove the app's Instagram access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations.

Your choices

Comments you post on YouTube or Instagram are governed by those platforms. You can edit or delete them there at any time. The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time at Google Account permissions. To ask about data relating to you, email khair0.humsi@gmail.com.

YouTube and Google policies

The app uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with the channel, you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Meta policies

Instagram activity is also subject to the Instagram Terms of Use and Meta's Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
